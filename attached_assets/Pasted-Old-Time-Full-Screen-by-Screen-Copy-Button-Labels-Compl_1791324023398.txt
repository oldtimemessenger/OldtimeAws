8. Complete Terms – Gifts, Membership, Live Streaming & Blue Badge
(Old Time Terms of Service – Section 12)
12.1 Virtual Gifts and Old Time Coins

Old Time Coins are a virtual currency used only inside the Old Time app. Coins have no cash value outside the platform and cannot be exchanged for real money except through official creator payouts. All purchases of Coins are final and non-refundable except where required by law.
12.2 Sending and Receiving Gifts

Users may send virtual gifts to other users during live streams or while the recipient is offline. Gifts are delivered instantly and cannot be reversed once sent. Abusive, harassing, or illegal messages attached to gifts may result in account restrictions or permanent suspension.
12.3 Creator Revenue Share

Creators with an active Old Time Membership receive 100% of the stated value of every gift.
Creators without an active Membership receive 70% of the stated value. The remaining 30% is retained by Old Time as a platform fee.
Revenue is calculated after any applicable taxes or payment-processor fees.

12.4 Membership

Old Time Membership is a paid monthly subscription. Benefits include unlimited live streaming, 100% gift revenue, eligibility for the Blue Badge, priority support, exclusive gift animations, and higher sending limits. Membership auto-renews unless cancelled. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are provided.
12.5 Blue Badge

The Blue Badge indicates a verified account. Verification requires accurate identity information and continued compliance with Old Time community guidelines. Old Time may remove the Blue Badge at any time for violations or false information.
12.6 Live Streaming

Membership holders may stream without time limits. Non-members are subject to daily live-streaming time limits. All live content must follow community guidelines. Old Time may end or restrict streams that violate the rules.
12.7 Age Restriction

Users under 18 may not purchase Old Time Coins or send paid gifts.
12.8 Changes to Pricing and Benefits

Old Time may change gift prices, revenue splits, membership benefits, or subscription pricing with reasonable prior notice. Continued use of the service after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the new terms.
12.9 Payouts

Eligible creators may request withdrawal of gift revenue subject to minimum thresholds, identity verification, and applicable tax requirements. Processing times and fees are shown in the payout section of the app.
