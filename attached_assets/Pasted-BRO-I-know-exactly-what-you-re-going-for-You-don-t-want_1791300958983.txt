BRO 😭🔥 I know exactly what you’re going for.

You don’t want to copy Snapchat. You want to take the community/discovery side of Snapchat and turn it into a much bigger social ecosystem: people + communities + interests + local life + chat rooms + discovery, all connected.

I’d structure it like this:

🏠 Main social experience

Your existing For You tab stays the main discovery feed.

Then users can move between:

* For You — algorithmic posts from people, communities, interests, and creators
* Following — people/accounts they follow
* Close Friends — more personal/private posts
* Communities — groups they join
* Nearby / City — local conversations and posts
* Interests — topics they selected

The important part: don’t make these feel like six completely separate apps. They should feed into one ecosystem.

👥 Community system

Anyone can create a community:

Fitness

* Gym
* Running
* Basketball
* Weightlifting
* Yoga

School

* College

* Study groups
* Specific schools/classes

City

* Miami
* Atlanta
* NYC
* Los Angeles
* Local neighborhoods

Interests

* Gaming
* Cars
* Music
* Photography
* Fashion
* Food
* Tech

A community can have:

Community Home

* Posts
* Stories
* Videos
* Discussions
* Members
* Events
* Chat rooms

And then:

Chat Rooms

* General
* Introductions
* Study Room
* Fitness Talk
* Random
* Live Room

So someone could join Miami Fitness, see posts from people in Miami who are into fitness, and jump into the Miami Fitness chat room.

That’s WAY more powerful than just following accounts.

Make rooms feel alive rather than like boring Discord channels.

Each room could show:

“Miami Heat Game — 9:00 PM”

“Sunday Run — 8:00 AM”

“Nursing Students — Finals Week”

The room can automatically disappear/archive afterward.

👻 Snapchat-style Stories

Keep Stories because they’re excellent for casual social interaction.

But let Stories exist inside communities too.

So:

Jaymes’ Story

and:

Miami Fitness Stories

Nursing Students Stories

Miami Tonight Stories

🔥 The really important part: Community → Feed → Chat

Everything should connect.

Someone makes a post:

“Anybody going to the gym tonight?”

It appears in Miami Fitness.

People react.

Someone taps:

💬 Discuss

→ opens the Miami Fitness chat room.

Someone else taps the person’s profile.

→ sees their interests.

→ follows them.

→ adds them to Close Friends later.

That’s the ecosystem.

🛡️ But you need strong privacy controls

Because you’re mixing strangers, cities, schools and interests.

Every community should have visibility settings:

Public
Anyone can discover/join.

Request to Join
Admin approves members.

Private
Invite only.

And users control whether their profile shows:

* City
* School
* Interests
* Communities
* Mutual communities
* Online status

Don’t expose someone’s precise location. City-level is enough.

⸻

And I’d make the navigation VERY simple

Since you already have your For You concept, I’d do something like:

For You | Communities | Chat | Create | Profile

Then inside For You, users get the Snapchat-style discovery experience.

Communities becomes the giant ecosystem.

Chat handles DMs + community rooms.

Create handles Posts / Story / Video / Community / Room / Event.

Profile handles identity, interests, communities, settings.

That gives you a social network that feels familiar like Snapchat, but the community layer makes it substantially deeper.

And honestly bro, the killer concept is:

Don’t just follow people. Follow places, interests, communities and conversations.

That’s what makes this feel like its own platform instead of “another Snapchat.”
