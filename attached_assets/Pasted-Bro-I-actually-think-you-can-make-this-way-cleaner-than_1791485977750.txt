Bro 😭 I actually think you can make this way cleaner than TikTok Duet if you stop thinking of it as “two videos side-by-side” and instead build it like a Remix Studio.

The key is: the original creator becomes a movable visual layer, and the person remixing controls where/how that layer appears.

The Remix Studio

When you tap Remix, open a full-screen editor with the original video already loaded.

Instead of locking the layout:

1. Split

* Original video on left/right.
* Drag the divider anywhere.
* 30/70, 50/50, 70/30, etc.
* Both videos stay perfectly framed.

2. Floating

* Original creator becomes a movable/resizable video window.
* Drag them anywhere.
* Pinch to resize.
* Rotate slightly if you want.
* Snap guides appear when they’re centered/aligned.

3. Background
This is the 🔥 one you’re describing.

The original person becomes the background video, almost like a Zoom/FaceTime presentation.

Example:

Original creator fills the screen
You appear in a clean foreground window

But you should have automatic subject separation so your camera feed can appear in front of the original video without looking like a rectangle.

Basically:

┌───────────────────────────┐
│                           │
│    ORIGINAL CREATOR       │
│                           │
│         🎥                │
│                           │
│              ┌─────────┐  │
│              │   YOU   │  │
│              │         │  │
│              └─────────┘  │
└───────────────────────────┘

And the user can drag YOU anywhere.

4. Picture-in-picture

Original video stays full screen.

Your camera becomes a floating bubble/card.

Give it professional snapping:

* top-left
* top-center
* top-right
* center-left
* center
* center-right
* bottom-left
* bottom-center
* bottom-right

But don’t make it look like a cheap FaceTime bubble.

Give the creator control over:

Shape

* rectangle
* rounded rectangle
* circle
* vertical card
* custom crop

Border

* none
* thin
* soft glow
* creator-style frame

Size

* small
* medium
* large
* custom

5. React

This could be your version of TikTok’s Duet reaction.

Original video plays normally.

Your camera appears as a foreground reaction layer.

But here’s where you can get crazy:

You could let the creator choose:

“Behind Me”

and automatically place the original video behind their body.

That gives you:

original video → background
user’s body → foreground

That’s much more visually impressive than standard Duet.

⸻

The thing I’d REALLY build

I’d make Remix use a layer-based compositor.

Think Photoshop/CapCut, but ridiculously simplified.

REMIX
────────────────────
[ ORIGINAL VIDEO ]  ← layer 1
[ YOUR CAMERA ]     ← layer 2
[ TEXT ]            ← layer 3
[ STICKER ]         ← layer 4
[ EFFECT ]          ← layer 5

The user doesn’t need to understand layers.

They just see:

Original
Me
Layout
Background
Effects

and everything updates live.

⸻

Make the transition itself feel premium

When they tap Remix:

Don’t immediately throw them into an ugly editor.

Animate the original video into the Remix canvas.

Something like:

Original video expands → gently shifts left → camera preview appears → controls fade in.

Then:

LAYOUT

Split       Floating
   ▣           ◉
Background    PiP
   ◉           ▣

Tap one and the composition smoothly morphs.

That makes the whole thing feel like a real creative tool rather than “TikTok copied.”

⸻

And here’s the killer feature

Freeform Remix

Don’t force the user to choose a preset.

They can literally grab the original video with their finger and move it.

For example:

Original creator starts full-screen.

User drags them:

      ┌───────────────┐
      │               │
      │   ORIGINAL    │
      │               │
      │          ┌────┤
      │          │YOU │
      └──────────┴────┘

Then pinch the original smaller.

Then move themselves.

Then record.

The layout becomes part of the video composition.

That’s the difference between:

TikTok Duet:
“Pick a layout.”

vs.

Old Time Remix:
“Build the scene.”

🔥

And I’d absolutely make Remix a first-class creation mode in Old Time rather than another little button buried inside the share menu.
