⚠️ One thing we need to get right

Don’t assume every website-to-app purchase redirect is automatically permitted.

For example, Apple’s rules for external purchase links differ depending on the storefront and applicable entitlements. Google also has specific requirements and exceptions governing external payment links.

The safest initial design is to explain the membership on the website and provide ordinary app download links, without directing users to an external checkout.

Your developer should verify the applicable policies before adding any purchase-specific redirects or external payment links.
