BRO, STOP AND REWORK THE CURRENT REMIX COMPOSITION LOGIC.

The current Remix implementation is NOT matching the product concept.

The idea is NOT simply:
“original video + my video side by side.”

The idea is:

I am responding to the content by appearing ON / INSIDE the content I’m talking about.

Think professional video compositing, not TikTok Duet.

IMPORTANT:
Do NOT throw away the existing Remix system. Inspect what is already implemented and correct the composition/rendering pipeline.

THE CORE EXPERIENCE

When I tap Remix on someone’s content:

The original content becomes the main visual environment.

My camera becomes a foreground subject that can be placed over that content.

For example:

ORIGINAL VIDEO
fills the screen

                ┌──────────┐
                │   ME     │
                │ talking  │
                └──────────┘

I can move myself around the original content.

I should be able to say:

“Look at THIS part…”

while my video is visually sitting on top of the content I’m talking about.

DEFAULT REMIX

Default mode should NOT be a 50/50 split.

Default should be:

Original content = background/main canvas
My camera = foreground layer

My camera should be a clean, movable subject/window.

SUBJECT MODE

This is critical.

My camera feed should support background removal / subject segmentation.

When background removal is enabled:

Do NOT show me as a rectangular video box.

Instead:

ORIGINAL CONTENT
↓
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│                             │
│      original content       │
│                             │
│            👤               │
│          ME                 │
│                             │
└─────────────────────────────┘

Only my actual body/person should appear.

The original content should remain visible around me.

MOVEMENT

I should be able to:

* drag myself anywhere
* pinch to resize
* reposition myself
* rotate slightly
* place myself over a specific part of the original video
* move myself away from important content
* quickly snap to useful positions

The original video remains underneath.

“POINT AT IT” EXPERIENCE

This is the important creative behavior.

If someone is talking about something happening in the original video, they should be able to position themselves next to it or directly over it.

Example:

Original video shows a car.

I can position myself beside the car and say:

“Look at this car right here.”

It should visually feel like I’m PRESENT in the content.

LAYOUT MODES

Keep multiple modes, but make the default concept clear:

1. OVERLAY
    My camera is layered over the original content.
2. CUTOUT
    My background is removed and only my person remains.
3. FLOATING
    My full camera feed becomes a movable window.
4. SPLIT
    Traditional side-by-side layout.
5. BACKGROUND
    Original content fills the scene while I appear in the foreground.

The user should be able to switch between these modes without leaving Remix.

PROFESSIONAL EDGE QUALITY

For CUTOUT/BACKGROUND mode:

The current subject extraction looks like a sticker.

Fix the actual alpha matte.

Do NOT fake this with:

* CSS blur
* drop shadows
* outlines
* thick feathering
* glowing borders

Use proper alpha-mask refinement.

The edge should have:

* natural feathering
* fine hair preservation
* clean fingers/hands
* clean clothing contours
* no white halo
* no dark halo
* no color contamination
* no jagged edges
* no obvious AI cutout boundary

Keep the inside of the subject sharp.

Only refine the boundary.

TEMPORAL VIDEO QUALITY

This is video, so the mask must remain stable.

Prevent:

* edge flickering
* mask crawling
* hair shimmering
* contour expansion/contraction
* visible frame-to-frame mask changes

Use temporal stabilization/tracking where appropriate.

COMPOSITING

The final composition should look like:

ORIGINAL CONTENT
+
MY REAL PRESENCE

Not:

VIDEO A
beside
VIDEO B

The viewer should immediately understand:

“I’m watching this person’s reaction/commentary INSIDE the content they’re responding to.”

IMPORTANT PRODUCT RULE

Do NOT make the user manually configure complicated technical settings.

The system should automatically produce a professional default composition.

The user should only need to:

1. Tap Remix
2. Camera opens
3. Their subject appears over the original content
4. Drag/resize themselves
5. Record

Everything else should be automatic.

VISUAL QUALITY TARGET

The result should feel closer to:

professional Photoshop-style compositing
+
CapCut-level video editing
+
Zoom/FaceTime-style presence

but with a much cleaner and simpler social-media workflow.

Do NOT copy TikTok Duet’s fixed composition.

Our differentiator is:

“Don’t just duet the content. Step into it.”

Before modifying anything, inspect the current Remix implementation and identify exactly why the current output is behaving like a normal two-video composition.

Fix the rendering/compositing architecture so the ORIGINAL CONTENT is the scene and MY VIDEO is the subject being composited into that scene.

Do not just change the UI labels. The actual rendered output must change.
