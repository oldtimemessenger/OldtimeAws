2. Full Community Guidelines Section
(Old Time Community Guidelines – Gifts, Live & Membership)
Respect & Safety

Do not send gifts or messages that harass, threaten, bully, or sexually exploit anyone.
Do not use gifts to spam, manipulate, or pressure other users.
Hate speech, discrimination, or content that promotes violence is not allowed in gift messages or live streams.

Live Streaming

You are responsible for everything that happens on your live stream.
Do not broadcast illegal activity, nudity, excessive violence, or dangerous challenges.
Do not encourage viewers to send gifts in exchange for anything illegal or harmful.
Old Time may end a live stream and restrict the account if guidelines are broken.

Gifts & Messages

Gift messages must follow the same rules as comments and posts.
Do not use anonymous gifts to harass or evade blocks.
Impersonation through gifts or fake Blue Badge claims is prohibited.

Membership & Blue Badge

Do not share membership accounts.
Providing false identity information for the Blue Badge will result in permanent removal of the badge and possible account suspension.
Buying or selling Blue Badges or memberships outside official channels is not allowed.

Consequences

Violations may result in:

Removal of specific gifts or messages
Temporary or permanent loss of live privileges
Removal of Blue Badge
Suspension or permanent ban of the account
Forfeiture of pending gift revenue in serious cases

Old Time reviews reports and takes action based on the severity and history of violations.

3. Complete Privacy Policy Language
(Old Time Privacy Policy – Gifts, Membership & Related Features)
Gifts and Virtual Currency

When you send or receive gifts, we collect and process:

The sender and recipient account IDs
Gift type, quantity, and coin value
Timestamp and whether the gift was sent during a live stream or offline
Optional message attached to the gift
Whether the gift was sent anonymously

This information is used to deliver the gift, calculate creator revenue, prevent fraud, and enforce our guidelines. Gift transaction records are retained for as long as your account is active and for a limited period afterward to meet legal and accounting requirements.
Membership

If you subscribe to Old Time Membership, we collect payment information (processed by our payment partners), subscription status, renewal dates, and cancellation requests. We use this data to provide membership benefits, manage billing, and send related notifications.
Blue Badge Verification

If you apply for the Blue Badge, we collect the identity information you submit (such as government ID or other verification documents) solely for the purpose of confirming authenticity. This data is stored securely and is not used for advertising. You may request deletion of verification documents after the review process is complete, subject to legal retention requirements.
Revenue and Payouts

For creators who receive gifts, we process revenue share calculations, payout requests, tax information (where required), and identity verification needed for withdrawals. Payout data is shared with our payment processors only as necessary to complete transfers.
Your Controls

You can:

Choose who is allowed to send you gifts
Turn gift notifications on or off
Control whether others can see that you received gifts
Block anonymous gifts
Cancel membership at any time
Request deletion of your gift history and verification data (subject to legal holds)

Data Sharing

We do not sell your gift or membership data. We share information only with:

Payment processors to complete transactions
Service providers who help us operate the gift and membership systems (under strict contracts)
Law enforcement or regulators when required by law

Children

Users under 18 cannot purchase Old Time Coins or send paid gifts. We do not knowingly collect payment or gift data from children under 13.
