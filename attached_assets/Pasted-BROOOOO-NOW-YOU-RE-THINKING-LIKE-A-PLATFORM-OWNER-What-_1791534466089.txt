3. 💰 THE RETURNING-USER SYSTEM

BRO, THIS IS WHERE YOU CAN MAKE THE APP FEEL PERSONAL. 😭

Instead of showing everybody the same subscription offer, Oldtimehive should recognize when someone returns after being inactive.

But the promotion should feel like a welcome-back perk, not a penalty for leaving.

How it works

Your backend or local eligibility logic tracks appropriate engagement events and calculates whether a user qualifies for a promotion.

For example:

User behavior	Possible response

The discounted price should only be displayed when the user actually qualifies and the store’s applicable offer is available.

Your dismissal system

This part matters.

Implement three dismissal choices:

* Not now: Hide the popup for a reasonable cooldown period.
* Don’t show this again: Suppress that specific campaign until the user explicitly reopens it or the offer materially changes.
* Already a member: Don’t show acquisition promotions to active members.

Store campaign dismissals against the user’s account when signed in, with local caching for responsiveness.

That way, dismissing an offer on one device can be respected on another.
