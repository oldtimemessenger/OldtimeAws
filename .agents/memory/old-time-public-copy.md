Internal technical writing such as “Device post migration” and “Retry text-post upload” must not be shown in public-facing screens.

Remove extra explanatory text across the app, including repeated device-storage/sync notices such as “Notes, media, and Blast chats stay on this device. They do not sync to other devices.”

**Why:** the user stated “these type of writings cannot be shown to public thats rules,” then repeated “investigate all these texts and make sure they deleted” and “remove all extra telling texts.”

**How to apply:** keep ordinary screens focused on content and controls, without technical banners or redundant explanatory paragraphs. Retain essential labels, concise errors, confirmations, and accurate unavailable-feature notices; do not imply working delivery, payments, or syncing when those capabilities are unavailable. Do not delete content or change underlying data behavior to hide a notice.

Use professional, action-specific social-app wording. A successful follow says “Following,” not the generic “Updated.” Distinguish an unfollow, a pending request and a canceled request.

**Why:** the user objected to “Updated” after following someone and asked for language like Facebook and Instagram.

**How to apply:** status messages should describe the actual completed action, using familiar concise labels rather than vague confirmations or casual explanatory copy.
