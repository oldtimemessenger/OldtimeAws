Avoid substituting a generic turn-arrow.

**Why:** the user rejected the earlier light Repost glyph and thin, turn-like Share symbol as not matching their reference.

**How to apply:** preserve the reference's broad right-facing arrowhead, thick lower-set shaft, smooth lower-left tail, and bold Repost weight when updating these icons.
