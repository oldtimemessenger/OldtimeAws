---
name: Old Time Close Friends prerequisite
description: Close Friends requires an existing confirmed regular friendship.
---

Someone must already be a confirmed Friend before they can be added to Close Friends. Following someone or having a pending Friend request is not enough.

**Why:** the user explicitly objected to adding people to Close Friends without being Friends first.

**How to apply:** enforce this prerequisite in Profile, Settings, saved membership, and Close Friends visibility checks. Unfriending must revoke Close Friends membership and access rather than leave an independent relationship behind.
