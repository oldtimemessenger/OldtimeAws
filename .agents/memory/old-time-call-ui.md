---
name: Old Time call UI
description: User-approved visual and copy constraints for call screens.
---

Use the other caller's real profile photo in the center of a full-screen call experience, with a softly blurred glass background tinted by Old Time's existing palette. Do not use Bitmoji or illustrated replacements, neon rings or glow, colors borrowed from reference screenshots, or Old Time branding in call and post-call text.

**Why:** the user explicitly asked for the real person photo, app colors, a FaceTime-like glass background, no glow, and removal of repeated Old Time wording from the call UI.

**How to apply:** keep call copy focused on call type, contact, status, and controls; preserve the live profile-photo binding and avoid repeated brand names.
